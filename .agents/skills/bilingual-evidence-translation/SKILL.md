# Bilingual Evidence Translation

Default output is complete Chinese and English sections. Keep original-language
evidence when its wording, a third language, a legal/financial term, or a
translation ambiguity materially affects the result.

Do not change numbers, signs, units, dates, currencies, tickers, identifiers,
or URLs. Label these separately: original quote, Chinese translation, English
mirror, translator note, and inference. Chinese is the primary reading language;
English is a complete mirror, not an optional summary.
